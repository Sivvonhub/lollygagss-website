Coming From Another Technician

If you are already wearing extensions and want to switch, message me first with a photo. Depending on retention and how the set was applied, I may be able to infill over it, or it may be cleaner to remove and start again. I would rather tell you that before you arrive than after.

If you have had a bad set before, stinging glue, sore eyes, or lashes that dropped within the first week, you are not alone. A lot of my lash clients came to me after exactly that experience somewhere else. Tell me what went wrong and I will work around it, whether that means a patch test, a different adhesive, or simply going slower.

Before Your Appointment

A few small things make a real difference to your result.

  • Arrive with clean lashes and no eye makeup, especially mascara. Removing it eats into your appointment time and old mascara ruins retention.
  • If you have a look in mind, a screenshot is the easiest way to show it. If not, we will map a set to your eye shape in the chair.
  • Plan infills every two to three weeks rather than waiting for the set to thin out.
  • Mention sensitive eyes or any past reaction to lash glue so I can patch test or adjust products before we start.

FAQ

Questions

How long do lash extensions last?
Two to four weeks before an infill, depending on your natural lash cycle and aftercare. Bali humidity affects retention more than most climates. See our humidity and retention guide.
Does it hurt?
No. Your eyes stay closed and the extensions attach to the lash, never the skin. Most clients fall asleep.
Can I wear mascara?
You will not need it, and oil-based products shorten retention. If you want more drama, go up a set rather than adding mascara.

Do I need to choose my lash set before my appointment?
