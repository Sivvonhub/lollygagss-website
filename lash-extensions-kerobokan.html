Lash Extensions in Kerobokan

Every set is applied by me, Lina. I own the studio and I am the only technician, so the person who maps your lash line is the same person who applies every extension and the same person you see at your infill four weeks later. That continuity is the whole point.

A full set takes between two and three and a half hours depending on the style. You are lying down the entire time with your eyes closed, so bring nothing and expect to be comfortable.

FAQ

Questions

How long do lash extensions last?
Two to four weeks before an infill, depending on your natural lash cycle and aftercare. Bali humidity affects retention more than most climates. See our humidity and retention guide.
Does it hurt?
No. Your eyes stay closed and the extensions attach to the lash, never the skin. Most clients fall asleep.
Can I wear mascara?
You will not need it, and oil-based products shorten retention. If you want more drama, go up a set rather than adding mascara.
