What BIAB Costs in Kerobokan

Pricing in Bali varies more than it should, and BIAB is sometimes quoted as a budget add-on and other times priced like a luxury treatment. At Lollygagss the price is set where the work sits: a proper builder application with a dry manicure and cuticle care, finished in the colour of your choice.

A full BIAB Manicure is Rp 325.000. There are no surprise extras for the standard service. If you want nail art on top of your BIAB, that is priced separately and I am happy to quote it before you commit.

02

How BIAB Differs From a Gel Manicure

A gel manicure is colour. It looks good for two to three weeks and then comes off. It does nothing structural.

BIAB is a structure that colour sits on top of. It typically lasts three to four weeks and, more usefully, it protects the nail underneath while it grows. Most clients who come in for BIAB are not chasing a look, they are trying to stop breaking nails.

If you only want colour and your nails are already strong, a Gel Manicure at Rp 195.000 is the better use of your money and I will tell you so.

03

What About Extensions

Gel Tip Extensions add length using a tip. BIAB does not add length. Same price, entirely different job. If you want longer nails now rather than in three months, extensions are the answer.

04

Upkeep

Come back every three to four weeks. At that appointment the overlay is filed back and rebalanced rather than removed, so the natural nail underneath keeps growing undisturbed.

05

Before Your Appointment

A few small things help you get the best result.

  • Come with bare nails if you can, or let us know in advance if you have an existing set that needs removing.
  • If you have a colour or look in mind, a screenshot is the easiest way to show it. If not, that is fine too.
  • Plan to rebook every three to four weeks as the nail grows, rather than waiting until there is obvious lifting.
  • Mention any history of lifting or weak nails so the prep can be adjusted to suit them.

FAQ

Questions

How long does BIAB last?
Three to four weeks for most clients, with a rebalance rather than a full removal at each appointment.
Does BIAB damage your nails?
No. It is an overlay on the natural nail and it is filed back rather than soaked off, so the nail underneath is protected while it grows.
Can I get nail art with BIAB?
Yes. Nail art is an add-on across four tiers, from Rp 60.000. Send a reference photo ahead so I can prepare the colours.

At Lollygagss Studio in Kerobokan, a BIAB Manicure is Rp 325.000 and includes a dry manicure, cuticle care, and the colour of your choice. Nail art can be added on top and is priced separately depending on the design.
Do I need to know what design I want before my BIAB appointment?
No. Many clients arrive with no fixed idea and decide on colour and finish in the chair. BIAB can be left clear as a strengthening overlay or finished in any colour, so you can keep it simple or build from there.
