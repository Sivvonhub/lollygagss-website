BIAB Nails in Kerobokan
Where to Book and What to Expect

You already know BIAB is the manicure that lasts in the tropics. This is the practical part: what it costs in Kerobokan, what your appointment actually looks like, and how to find us.

By Lina Zahirah Dimes
Studio Teuku Umar, Kerobokan
Published June 2026

Most people searching for BIAB in Kerobokan have already done the homework. You know it stands for Builder In A Bottle, you know it strengthens the natural nail, and you have probably had it before in Australia, the UK, or somewhere else in Bali. What you actually want now is somewhere reliable to book it close to home.

So this is the practical guide. If you are still weighing up whether BIAB is the right service for you, we cover that in our piece on what BIAB is and why it works in Bali's heat. Here, the focus is simpler: getting it done well, in Kerobokan, by the same person every time.

Where to get BIAB nails in Kerobokan

Lollygagss Studio sits on Jl. Persada II, just off Teuku Umar Barat in Kerobokan Kelod. It is a quiet side street rather than a busy main road, which is part of the point. You are not walking into a high-turnover salon with five chairs going at once. It is a small studio where one technician does the work and the appointment is yours.

Teuku Umar is central in a way that suits a lot of clients. It is close enough to Seminyak and Umalas for a quick trip, easy to reach from Kuta and Legian, and a sensible distance from Denpasar without the parking headache of the tourist strips. For most of Badung's expat and local community, it is one of the more practical places to keep a regular nail appointment.

What BIAB costs in Kerobokan

Pricing in Bali varies more than it should, and BIAB is sometimes quoted as a budget add-on and other times priced like a luxury treatment. At Lollygagss the price is set where the work sits: a proper builder application with a dry manicure and cuticle care, finished in the colour of your choice.

A full BIAB Manicure is Rp 250.000, which includes a dry manicure, cuticle care, and the colour of your choice. There are no surprise extras for the standard service. Most clients rebook every three to four weeks as the nail grows, which keeps things looking their best. If you want nail art on top of your BIAB, that is priced separately and I am happy to quote it before you commit.

Why clients choose Lollygagss for BIAB

There are plenty of places to get your nails done around Teuku Umar. What makes this one different is simple: you see the same person every time. I am the owner and the only technician, so there is no rotation, no being handed to a junior halfway through, and no pressure to add things you did not come in for. You book with me, and I am the one who does your nails from start to finish.

The studio is small on purpose. I take one client at a time in a calm, clean space rather than running several chairs at once, so your appointment is unhurried and the prep is done properly. With BIAB in particular, careful prep is most of what decides how long it lasts, and that is exactly the part a busy salon tends to rush.

Getting to the studio

Bali is small, and Teuku Umar is well connected, so most clients reach us in under twenty minutes. Here is roughly what to expect from the nearby areas, traffic depending.

Approximate travel to Lollygagss Studio
From Seminyak
Around 10 to 15 minutes by scooter or car, traffic depending.
From Umalas
A short hop, usually under 10 minutes.
From Kuta or Legian
Roughly 15 to 20 minutes via Sunset Road or Teuku Umar.
From Denpasar
About 15 minutes, with easier parking than the beach areas.

If you would rather not travel, we also offer home service for clients staying nearby. If you are in a villa around Seminyak or Kerobokan and would prefer your appointment to come to you, message us on WhatsApp to check availability for your dates.

How to book

There are two easy ways to book BIAB at Lollygagss. You can reserve online through Fresha at a time that suits you, or message me directly on WhatsApp if you would rather ask a question first or sort out a home service booking. Either way it comes straight to me.

Before you come in, it helps to keep a few small things in mind for the best result.

  • Come with bare nails if you can, or let us know in advance if you have an existing set that needs removing.
  • If you have a colour or look in mind, a screenshot is the easiest way to show it. If not, that is fine too.
  • Plan to rebook every three to four weeks as the nail grows, rather than waiting until there is obvious lifting.
  • Mention any history of lifting or weak nails so the prep can be adjusted to suit them.

Appointments are available Monday through Saturday, 09:00 to 19:00, with Sundays by request. Whether you are a Kerobokan local or just here for a longer stay, you are welcome at the studio.
