Lash Extensions in Kerobokan
Where to Book and What to Expect

You know roughly the set you want. This is the practical part: what lash extensions cost in Kerobokan, what your appointment actually looks like, and how to find us.

By Lina Zahirah Dimes
Studio Teuku Umar, Kerobokan
Published July 2026

If you are searching for lash extensions in Kerobokan, you are not short of options. This corner of Bali has more lash studios per square kilometre than almost anywhere on the island, and most of them will happily take your booking today. What is harder to find is consistency: the same technician, the same products, and the same result every time you come back.

So this is the practical guide to booking with me. If you are still deciding which set suits you, start with my guide to Classic, Volume and Style lashes. And if you are wondering how long a set actually holds up in this climate, I have written honestly about lash retention in Bali humidity. Here, the focus is simpler: getting your lashes done well, in Kerobokan, by the same person every time.

Where to get lash extensions in Kerobokan

Lollygagss Studio sits on Jl. Persada II, just off Teuku Umar Barat in Kerobokan Kelod. It is a quiet side street rather than a busy main road, and for lash work that matters more than you might think. A full set takes around two hours with your eyes closed, longer for a Style set, and the difference between a calm private room and a salon with five chairs going at once is the difference between a nap and an endurance test.

Teuku Umar is central in a way that suits a lot of clients. It is close enough to Seminyak and Umalas for a quick trip, easy to reach from Kuta and Legian, and a sensible distance from Denpasar without the parking headache of the tourist strips. If you keep a regular infill schedule, and with lashes you should, it is one of the more practical places in Badung to keep that appointment.

What lash extensions cost in Kerobokan

Lash pricing in Bali runs from suspiciously cheap to resort-level expensive, and the price does not always tell you much about the work. At Lollygagss the menu is short and the pricing is on the page, so you know exactly what you are booking before you arrive.

A Classic set is Rp 250.000, Volume is Rp 350.000, and Style sets, the wispy and spiky looks you have probably saved on Instagram, are Rp 450.000. Infills run from Rp 150.000 to Rp 350.000 depending on your set, and I recommend booking them every two to three weeks. A Classic or Volume set takes around two hours and a Style set takes three and a half, so plan your day around it rather than squeezing it between other appointments.

Why clients choose Lollygagss for lashes

Lash extensions ask for more trust than most beauty services. Your eyes are closed for a couple of hours while someone works a few millimetres from them, and you cannot check the result until it is finished. That is exactly why seeing the same technician every time matters. I am the owner and the only technician at Lollygagss, so the person who mapped your last set, knows your natural lashes, and remembers what worked is the same person doing your infill.

I take one client at a time in a calm, clean space. With lashes in particular, unhurried work shows. Proper isolation of each natural lash is what separates a set that grows out comfortably from one that tangles, pulls, and damages your own lashes, and that careful application is the first thing a high-turnover salon cuts. It is the part I refuse to rush.

Getting to the studio

Bali is small, and Teuku Umar is well connected, so most clients reach us in under twenty minutes. Here is roughly what to expect from the nearby areas, traffic depending.

If you would rather not travel, I also offer home service for clients staying nearby. If you are in a villa around Seminyak or Kerobokan and would prefer your lash appointment to come to you, message me on WhatsApp to check availability for your dates.

How to book

There are two easy ways to book lashes at Lollygagss. You can reserve online through Fresha at a time that suits you, or message me directly on WhatsApp if you would rather ask a question first or arrange a home service booking. Either way it comes straight to me.

Before you come in, a few small things make a real difference to your result.

  • Arrive with clean lashes and no eye makeup, especially mascara. Removing it eats into your appointment time and old mascara ruins retention.
  • If you have a look in mind, a screenshot is the easiest way to show it. If not, we will map a set to your eye shape in the chair.
  • Plan infills every two to three weeks rather than waiting for the set to thin out.
  • Mention sensitive eyes or any past reaction to lash glue so I can patch test or adjust products before we start.

Appointments are available Monday through Saturday, 09:00 to 19:00, with Sundays by request. Whether you are a Kerobokan local or here for a longer stay, you are welcome at the studio.
