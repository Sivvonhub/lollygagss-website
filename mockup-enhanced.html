Our Story

Lollygagss Studio

Lollygagss Studio is a boutique nail and lash studio nestled in Teuku Umar, right where the warmth of local Bali life meets the energy of Kerobokan's expat community. Opened in November 2024, the studio is owner-operated and every appointment is handled personally, ensuring a consistent, attentive experience from start to finish.

We offer a full range of nail services including gel manicure, BIAB, nail extensions, and nail art from simple to intricate designs, alongside professional lash extensions from natural 2D through to volume 6D.

Lollygagss welcomes everyone: Bali locals, tourists making the most of the island, and the expats who came for a holiday and never quite left.
