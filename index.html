Our Story

Lollygagss Studio

I'm Lina, and Lollygagss Studio is my nail and lash studio in Kerobokan Kelod, on a quiet side street just off Teuku Umar Barat. I opened in November 2024 and I am the only technician here, so the person you book with is the person who does your nails, every time.

For nails I offer gel manicures, BIAB builder overlay, gel tip extensions, and nail art across four tiers, from simple dots and lines through to hand-painted 3D work. For lashes, Classic, Volume and Style sets, along with infills and gentle removal.

Booking ahead is the best way to get the time you want, though walk-ins are welcome if I have a gap. Home service is available across Kerobokan, Seminyak and Umalas. Everyone is welcome here, whether you live in Bali, you are visiting, or you came for a holiday and never quite left.
