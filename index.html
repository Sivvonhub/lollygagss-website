Our Story

Lollygagss Studio

Lollygagss Studio was born from a passion for beautiful nails and the belief that every woman deserves to feel her best. Located in Teuku Umar, Bali, we offer precision nail and lash services in a calm, clean, and welcoming space.

We work by appointment only, ensuring every client receives our full, unhurried attention. Home service is also available for those who prefer the comfort of their own space.
